Services

United Rehabilitation & Chiropractor Clinic is committed on educating patients on post injury care as we help relieve physical pain. Wellness recovery is our focus for individuals suffering from chronic pain, sport injuries, car accidents, slip & falls, work incidents.

Chiropractic Adjustment

A technique used to correct misalignments and dysfunctions in the spine and other joints. The goal is to restore proper alignment and function, which can help alleviate pain, improve mobility, and enhance overall health.

Sport Massage

A specialized form of massage therapy designed to enhance athletic performance, prevent injuries, and aid in recovery. It's tailored to the needs of athletes and active individuals, addressing both the physical demands of their activities and their specific muscular and skeletal issues.

Physical Therapy & Rehab

Integral components of recovery and wellness for individuals dealing with injuries, surgeries, chronic conditions, or movement disorders. They involve a range of techniques and exercises aimed at improving physical function, reducing pain, and enhancing overall quality of life.

Car Accident

Chiropractic treatment can be an effective approach for managing injuries sustained in car accidents, particularly those involving musculoskeletal issues such as whiplash, back pain, and neck pain.

Slip and Fall

Slip and fall accidents can lead to a variety of injuries, including back pain, neck pain, and soft tissue damage. Chiropractors use a range of techniques to alleviate symptoms, promote healing, and restore function.
